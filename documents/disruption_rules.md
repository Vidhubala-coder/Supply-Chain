# Operational Disruption Rules

## Section 1: Factory Fire & Production Halt Protocol
When a supplier notifies of a factory fire or critical production halt:
1. Immediately audit all pending orders depending on affected SKUs.
2. Determine available unreserved stock on hand.
3. Freeze non-essential shipments to preserve stock for priority orders.

## Section 2: Carrier Congestion & Port Delays
For sea-freight vessel delays exceeding 10 days, operations must evaluate air-freight replacement for backordered line items.
