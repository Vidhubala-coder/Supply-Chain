# Global Supply Chain Policy

## Section 1: Customer Tier Prioritization
VIP customer orders must be protected using all available safety stock and expedited freight options whenever a disruption threatens delivery dates by more than 3 days. Standard tier customer orders should be managed via partial shipments or proactive delivery adjustments to minimize cost impact.

## Section 2: Inventory Allocation & Safety Stock Rules
Warehouse safety stock is intended to absorb supply chain volatility. Safety stock allocation must be authorized when customer order shortfall exceeds 20% of order quantity.

## Section 3: Cost Authorization Limits
Logistics coordinators may authorize expedite air freight costs up to $500 per order without manager escalation. Expedite requests exceeding $500 require escalation to the Supply Chain Manager.
