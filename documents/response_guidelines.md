# Disruption Response Guidelines

## Section 1: Expedite Shipment Criteria
Air freight expediting is recommended when customer order value exceeds $2,500 and estimated delivery delay exceeds 7 days.

## Section 2: Part-Shipment Protocol
If partial stock is available (at least 30% of order quantity), dispatch available units immediately to mitigate customer downtime, placing the remaining balance on backorder.

## Section 3: Customer Delay Notification
When no stock is available and expediting is cost-prohibitive, notify customer within 24 hours and issue a 5% goodwill discount credit.
